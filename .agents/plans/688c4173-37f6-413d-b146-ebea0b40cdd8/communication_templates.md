# RetailNode Enterprise Communication Architecture

This document outlines the **Advanced Enterprise Scale** communication templates for RetailNode. This architecture accounts for Omni-Channel sales, Warehouses & Godowns, Multi-Level Employee workflows, Granular Inventory, and Rich HTML Email alerts with PDF attachments.

---

## 1. Omni-Channel & End-Customer Engagement

### Digital E-Invoice
**Trigger:** Sales Bill generated successfully
**WhatsApp / SMS Content:**
```text
Dear {{customer_name}}, 
Thank you for shopping with us! Your invoice #{{bill_no}} for the amount of {{amount}} has been generated.
You can view and download your E-Bill here: {{invoice_pdf_link}}
Regards, {{firm_name}}
```
**Email Content (Rich HTML):**
```html
<div style="font-family: Arial, sans-serif;">
  <h2>Thank you for your purchase, {{customer_name}}!</h2>
  <p>Please find the details of your recent transaction below, and your official invoice attached as a PDF.</p>
  <table border="1" cellpadding="10" cellspacing="0" style="width: 100%; border-collapse: collapse;">
    <tr style="background-color: #f3f4f6;">
      <th>Item</th><th>Qty</th><th>Rate</th><th>Total</th>
    </tr>
    {{#each invoice_items}}
    <tr>
      <td>{{item_name}}</td><td>{{qty}}</td><td>{{rate}}</td><td>{{total}}</td>
    </tr>
    {{/each}}
    <tr style="font-weight: bold;">
      <td colspan="3" align="right">Grand Total</td><td>{{amount}}</td>
    </tr>
  </table>
  <br>
  <p>Regards,<br><strong>{{firm_name}}</strong></p>
</div>
<!-- Attachments: invoice_{{bill_no}}.pdf -->
```

### Omni-Channel Order Confirmation (E-com / POS / Marketplace)
**Trigger:** Order placed via Website, App, POS, or Marketplace sync.
**Channel:** WhatsApp / Email
```text
Dear {{customer_name}}, 
Thank you for your order #{{order_id}} via {{channel_name}}!
Items: {{style_names_list}}
Amount: {{total_amount}}

Track your order here: {{tracking_link}}
Regards, {{firm_name}} - {{location_name}} Branch
```

### Buy Online, Pick-Up In Store (BOPIS) Ready Alert
**Trigger:** Store marks online order as packed and ready for pickup.
**Channel:** WhatsApp / SMS
```text
Hello {{customer_name}}, 
Good news! Your order #{{order_id}} is packed and ready for pickup at our {{location_name}} store. 
Please bring this message and a valid ID.
```

## 2. Warehouses, Godowns & Bulk Logistics

### Bulk Warehouse Inward / GRN (Goods Receipt Note)
**Trigger:** Goods arrive at a primary Warehouse or Godown from a Vendor/Manufacturer.
**Channel:** Email / Dashboard Alert (To Procurement Team)
```text
Warehouse Alert: Bulk Inward Received

Warehouse/Godown: {{godown_name}}
GRN No: {{grn_number}}
Against PO: #{{po_number}}
Total Bales/Boxes: {{box_count}}

The goods have been received at the godown and are pending QC (Quality Check) and barcoding.
```

### Godown to Store Dispatch (Stock Transfer Outward)
**Trigger:** Warehouse/Godown dispatches stock to a retail storefront.
**Channel:** WhatsApp (To Store Manager)
```text
Dispatch Alert from Central Godown

Hello {{store_manager}}, 
Stock Transfer #{{transfer_id}} has been dispatched from {{godown_name}} to your location ({{store_name}}).
Vehicle/Transporter: {{vehicle_number}}
Total Items: {{item_count}} ({{category_name}})

Please ensure inwarding upon arrival.
```

## 3. Procurement & Vendor Management

### Purchase Order (PO) Dispatch
**Trigger:** PO approved and finalized
**WhatsApp Content:**
```text
Dear {{vendor_name}}, 
Please find our Purchase Order #{{po_number}} for {{total_amount}}. View PO Document: {{po_pdf_link}}
Regards, {{firm_name}}
```
**Email Content (Rich HTML):**
```html
<div style="font-family: Arial, sans-serif;">
  <h2>Purchase Order #{{po_number}}</h2>
  <p>Dear {{vendor_name}}, please process the following order and confirm the dispatch schedule.</p>
  <table border="1" cellpadding="10" cellspacing="0" style="width: 100%; border-collapse: collapse;">
    <tr style="background-color: #f3f4f6;">
      <th>SKU / Item</th><th>Order Qty</th><th>Rate</th><th>Amount</th>
    </tr>

    <tr>
      <td>{{sku}} - {{item_name}}</td><td>{{qty}}</td><td>{{rate}}</td><td>{{amount}}</td>

  <p>Regards,<br>{{firm_name}} Procurement Team</p>
</div>
<!-- Attachments: PO_{{po_number}}.pdf -->
```

## 4. Multi-Level Approvals & Workflow (Multi-Employee)

### Purchase Order (PO) Approval Request (Level 1 / Level 2)
**Trigger:** Employee generates a PO that requires Manager/Director approval.
**Channel:** Internal Email / App Notification
```text
Action Required: PO Approval #{{po_number}}
Generated By: {{employee_name}} ({{location_name}})
Vendor: {{vendor_name}}
Amount: {{po_amount}}

This PO belongs to the {{department_name}} department. 
Click here to Approve or Reject: {{approval_link}}
```

### Credit Limit / Discount Override Request
**Trigger:** Floor salesman tries to give a discount beyond their authorized limit.
**Channel:** WhatsApp (To Store Manager)
```text
Override Request: 
Salesman {{employee_name}} at {{location_name}} is requesting approval for a {{discount_percent}}% discount on Bill #{{draft_bill_no}}. 
Customer: {{customer_name}}

Reply APPROVE or REJECT, or click: {{override_link}}
```

## 5. Departmental Budgeting & End of Day

### Daily EOD Sales Digest
**Trigger:** 11:59 PM cron job
**WhatsApp Content:**
```text
RetailNode EOD Report: {{firm_name}}
Total Sales: {{total_sales}} | Cash: {{cash_collected}} | Returns: {{returns}}
```
**Email Content (Rich HTML):**
```html
<div style="font-family: Arial, sans-serif;">
  <h2>Daily Sales Digest: {{firm_name}} ({{date}})</h2>
  <table border="1" cellpadding="10" cellspacing="0" style="width: 100%; border-collapse: collapse;">
    <tr style="background-color: #f3f4f6;">
      <th>Location / Branch</th><th>Sales Amount</th><th>Cash Collected</th><th>Returns</th>
    </tr>
    {{#each location_metrics}}
    <tr>
      <td>{{location_name}}</td><td>{{sales}}</td><td>{{cash}}</td><td>{{returns}}</td>
    </tr>
    {{/each}}
    <tr style="font-weight: bold; background-color: #e2e8f0;">
      <td>Firm Total</td><td>{{total_sales}}</td><td>{{cash_collected}}</td><td>{{total_returns}}</td>
    </tr>
  </table>
  <p><em>A detailed breakdown is attached as a PDF report.</em></p>
</div>
<!-- Attachments: EOD_Report_{{date}}.pdf -->
```

### Purchase Budget Warning (80% Utilized)
**Trigger:** A PO pushes a Department's monthly purchase budget past 80%.
**Channel:** Email (To Finance / Department Head)
```text
Budget Alert: {{department_name}} - {{section_name}}

Hello {{manager_name}},
The recent PO #{{po_number}} has pushed the {{department_name}} purchase budget to {{utilized_percent}}% utilization for {{month_year}}.
Remaining Budget: {{remaining_budget}}

Please review future procurement carefully.
```
